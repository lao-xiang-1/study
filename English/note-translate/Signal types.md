
## A. Unit Step Signal

The unit step signal is commonly used to model physical processes that start abruptly, such as flipping a switch.

It is defined as:
$$
u(t) = \begin{cases} 
1, & t > 0 \\
0, & t < 0 
\end{cases}
$$

- At t=0, the signal undergoes a jump discontinuity, its value at that point is usually considered undefined, though some textbooks define it as $1/2$.
