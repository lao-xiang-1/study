Throughout my primary school years, I didn't have much trouble most of the time. After all, I was just a kid, I didn't think too much about life or anything. And most of the things that made me unhappy back then don't seem like a big deal in my perspective now.

Since a general expression doesn't show my feelings correctly, I choose to write some independent stories

##### A
It happened when I was in first grade. My sister, who was five years older that me, was at the same school as me and was already sixth grade. Because our parents were too busy to pick us up, we stayed at an after-school care center.
Around that time, one of my sister's friends was having a birthday party. My sister and her friends had put together a little performance to celebrate it, and they needed a kid to play one of the roles. There were two of us to choose from -- me and my friend. After some sort of try-out, they picked my friend instead.
At the party itself, the performance went well with my friend in the part. As for me, I had no gift to give. I just stood there and cried.
Afterwards, the teacher of our after-school care center conforted me with gentle words. I calmed down soon.

##### B
Because I was in the after-school care, at that environment, you know, you have to play with others to get into the environment, so I often played with other students in the  after-school care. And I was bad at play games, I often failed and at most of the time, I just cried in place. Sometimes they didn't want to play with me.

##### C
There is a series of street food markets near my school. Many students buy food there.
They had thier own pocket money, but I didn't have money. It's not because my family is poor or anything, I think I can get money if ask. But whatever, I didn't ask my parents for money. So I didn't often go to that place. 
Then someday, I got some money somehow (I don't remenber where the money came from, but I'm sure it was related to my one classmate). I went to buy food with that friend. We went to a food market that sells soup noodles, it is filled in a hand-size plastic cup. But I was too shy to talk to the owner. I don't know why but I was just standing in that place for a few seconds without responding to my friend's voice. And then we went back, to a normal snack market, and buy a potato chip. The reason is I don't need to talk to owner, just passing the money is OK.

##### F
The reward system built by my class teacher and one classmate took advantages of it.

At low grades, I have troubles only with my classmates, and it's really not a big deal. 



But at high grades , things got different. 

First thing is about my English teacher. She is a strict teacher that ask high standards for students. She often kept me late at school to recite the text and vocabulary. It didn't aim at just me, but I was often left late. However, not like other people that was left, I was actually not bad at English. The reason she choose that student to leave is that he not finishing the task that recite the content. Maybe I just didn't want to repeate text to my teacher. I was too shy or I pushed myself to remember the text perfectly and only then can I talk to my teacher.

Second is about my math, not math teacher, just math. However, it's somehow related to my math teacher. At that time, I was good at math at 5th grade, and there is one more math-good students in my class, and other students thought he was the best. But my math teacher didn't think that way, he told everyone in the classroom that I was actually best at math in class. I didn't know his real thought until now. But the action is bad for a kid in that age I think. But anyway, my math got bad since that time

Third is about my class teacher. In China, we are told in our young ages that we should study hard to get good grades. In 6th grade, it is hard for me to remenber things all of a sudden. I don't know why but at that time my class teacher talk to me about it personally. It is a strict talk for me back then. And i thought it was my fault.

My stutter began when I was in sixth grade at primary school, and it hasn't improved since.
